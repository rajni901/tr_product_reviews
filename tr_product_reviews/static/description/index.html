⚙️ Configuration Steps

1

Install the Module

Go to Apps → Search "Product Reviews" → Install

After installation, the reviews section automatically appears on all product pages.

2

Customer Submits a Review

  • Customer visits any product page on your website
  • Scrolls down to "Customer Reviews" section
  • Must be logged in — a login link is shown if not
  • Selects 1–5 stars by clicking the stars
  • Enters a Review Title (required)
  • Writes their Review (optional)
  • Clicks "Submit Review"
  • A toast notification confirms submission
3

Admin Reviews Pending Submissions

  • Go to Website → eCommerce → Products → Product Reviews
  • By default shows Pending reviews
  • View Product, Customer, Star Rating, Title, Date
  • Click ✓ Approve — review goes live on website immediately
  • Click ✗ Reject — review is hidden from website
4

Review Shows on Product Page

  • After approval, refresh the product page
  • Review appears in the Customer Reviews section
  • Average rating and review count update automatically
  • Reviewer name and date are shown
5

Enable / Disable Reviews Per Product

  • Go to Website → eCommerce → Products
  • Open any product
  • Click "Reviews & Ratings" tab
  • Toggle "Allow Reviews" ON or OFF
  • When OFF — the reviews section is hidden for that product
6

View Review Stats on Product

  • Open any product form → Reviews & Ratings tab
  • See Average Rating and Review Count
  • View and manage all reviews for that product directly
  • Approve or reject reviews without leaving the product form
